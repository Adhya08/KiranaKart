# KiranaKart Privacy Policy (DPDP Act, 2023 Compliant)

**Effective Date:** 14 November 2026  
**Applicability:** Customers, Store Owners, Delivery Personnel, Admin Staff

## 1. Statutory Context
This Privacy Policy outlines KiranaKart's policies regarding the collection, processing, storage, protection, and disposal of digital personal data in strict compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and rules notified thereunder.

## 2. Core Principles
1. **Notice & Granular Consent:** Separate, unbundled choices for all non-essential purposes.
2. **Purpose Limitation:** Data is processed solely for stated, consented purposes.
3. **Data Minimisation:** Only fields essential to fulfill grocery orders are collected.
4. **Reasonable Security Safeguards:** Cryptographic protection at rest (AES-256-GCM), secure transit (TLS 1.3), rate-limiting, and principle of least privilege.
5. **Data Principal Rights:** Free self-service access, correction, erasure, nomination, and grievance redressal.
6. **Child Protection:** Strict age-gating, verifiable parental consent, and absolute ban on child profiling/tracking.
7. **Storage Localization:** All primary production data resides within sovereign Indian territory.

## 3. Data Categories & Retention
See `privacy/retention_schedule.json` for granular retention thresholds. Data is purged automatically when purposes are fulfilled or upon erasure request.
