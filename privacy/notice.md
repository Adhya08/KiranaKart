# KiranaKart Privacy Notice (DPDP Act, 2023 - Section 5)

**Notice Version:** 2026.1  
**Last Updated:** 14 November 2026  
**Data Fiduciary:** KiranaKart Technologies Private Limited  

---

## 1. Introduction
This Notice is provided in compliance with Section 5 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. KiranaKart ("we", "our", "us") collects and processes personal data strictly necessary to provide hyperlocal grocery delivery services.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect & Specific Purposes
In accordance with Section 4 and Section 6 of the DPDP Act, your consent is requested separately for the following granular purposes:

| Purpose Category | Data Fields Collected | Lawful Basis | Necessity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Essential Service Fulfillment** | Name, Phone, Delivery Address, Location Coordinates, Order Items | Consent (Section 6) / Contractual Necessity | Required for cart delivery and billing |
| **Order Status Notifications** | Phone number (SMS/WhatsApp) | Consent (Section 6) | Optional: Dispatches, ETAs, Delivery OTP |
| **Promotions & Offers** | Email, Phone | Consent (Section 6) | Optional: Kirana discounts, seasonal campaigns |
| **Performance Analytics** | Anonymized device telemetry, crash reports | Consent (Section 6) | Optional: UI responsiveness, checkout reliability |

## 3. Children's Personal Data (Section 9)
Users under the age of 18 are identified at registration. In accordance with Section 9(1) of the DPDP Act, processing of a minor's data requires **verifiable parental consent**. In accordance with Section 9(3), we strictly prohibit behavioural monitoring, tracking, or targeted marketing directed at minors.

## 4. Your Rights as a Data Principal
Under Chapter III (Sections 11 to 14) of the DPDP Act, you possess the following rights:
1. **Right to Access (Section 11):** Summary of personal data processed and identities of data processors shared with. (Use "Download My Data" in Privacy Center).
2. **Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12):** Update outdated profile fields or request complete erasure of your personal data.
3. **Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13):** Submit a grievance to our Grievance Officer with guaranteed acknowledgement and 72-hour turnaround.
4. **Right to Nominate (Section 14):** Designate a nominee to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
5. **Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4)):** Revoke optional consents at any time in the Privacy Center without affecting the legality of prior processing.
